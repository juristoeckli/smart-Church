# Elvanto integration

## Current implementation

The Sync settings panel can connect an Elvanto account, verify its API key with a one-record People read, encrypt and store that key for the selected location, and save a direction choice for each planned data area. Direction choices are independent: disabled, Elvanto to Smart Church, Smart Church to Elvanto, or bidirectional. The all-area presets apply to People, People Flows, Groups, Services, Songs, and Calendar. Financial/giving remains disabled unless a site administrator explicitly enables it; the financial direction stays off if that permission is unchecked.

The settings panel also has a read-only People import preview when People is directed from Elvanto to Smart Church. It pages through People (up to 200 pages of 1,000 records), compares stable Elvanto IDs with already linked contacts, and reports likely creates, existing links, email matches, ambiguous email matches, and records needing review. It returns up to 50 sample names and actions to an authorized location administrator. The preview also reads the complete custom-field definition list and requests optional profile values for birthday, gender, anniversary, school grade, marital status, address, demographics, service types, and locations. A complete field list and People scan are both required for a complete preview. The field definitions are stored with the preview and revalidated on every import batch; if they change, the preview is invalidated so a resumed batch cannot silently apply a different field mapping. Email matches are never merged automatically. If the API reports more records than were scanned, the preview is marked incomplete. A complete preview is required for import, expires after one hour, and is invalidated when connection settings change. The preview makes no contact changes.

After a complete preview, an administrator can import eligible contacts. Each request processes at most five Elvanto pages (5,000 people), and the next page and counts are saved transactionally so the administrator can resume later. Settings changes and disconnect are blocked while a batch is running. When the initial import is complete, the same button starts a full People refresh from page one; stable IDs make it safe to repeat, and no contacts are deleted. New contacts are stored with `source_system='elvanto'` and the Elvanto person ID; linked Elvanto contacts have name, email, phone, and the local member status refreshed. The Elvanto profile snapshot preserves preferred/middle names, account status, archived/contact/volunteer flags, family identifiers and relationships, optional profile fields, list-valued assignments, and each custom field's ID, label, type, and value. The local `member`/`visitor` flag remains separate from Elvanto's account status. Imported profile details are returned only to users who can view private People data. Financial-only values and security codes are not requested by the People importer. Email matches, malformed or missing names, and duplicate source IDs are skipped; they are not automatically merged. Elvanto-linked contacts are shown as read-only in the People directory and cannot be edited or deleted through the normal contact endpoints. This importer remains one-way and does not yet do incremental change detection or export.

People can also be exported from Smart Church to Elvanto when that direction is enabled. The preview scans local contacts and the full Elvanto People list, then classifies creates, updates for contacts linked by `elvanto_source_id`, and email or missing-link conflicts that need manual review. The preview expires after one hour and saves a hash of each local record and linked Elvanto record. Before each write, the exporter checks that the local record has not changed; it also rereads linked Elvanto records and checks for a new Elvanto email match before creating a person. A changed record or new collision invalidates the preview. Each request writes at most five contacts and examines at most 100 preview rows, saving progress so an administrator can resume. Only first name, last name, email, and phone are mapped. Other Elvanto profile fields, volunteer/contact flags, family data, custom fields, deletion, and archival are not written. Email matches, duplicate local emails, and contacts whose linked Elvanto record is missing are skipped for manual resolution. This is a reviewed People export slice, not full bidirectional synchronization.

A read-only Groups preview pages through up to 200,000 Elvanto groups and includes each group's people list. It reports new groups, already linked groups, same-name conflicts, invalid rows, membership matches, and existing local-only memberships. A complete preview must be less than one hour old before import. Each request imports at most five pages and saves its page and counts transactionally, so it can resume. Existing Elvanto groups refresh their description and add or update memberships linked to Elvanto people; local-only memberships are kept and never removed automatically. Elvanto membership positions become local group-member roles. Memberships link to contacts already linked to an Elvanto person ID, either through import or export; unmatched people are skipped and shown in the preview. Local groups with a matching name are skipped for manual resolution instead of being merged. Elvanto groups and memberships are read-only in the normal Groups workflows. Groups absent from Elvanto are not deleted, and public signup and local meeting attendance are not supported by this adapter.

Groups can also be exported from Smart Church to Elvanto. A complete preview scans local groups and Elvanto groups with member lists, checks stable group/person links, flags name collisions and missing People links, and reports memberships that exist only in Elvanto. The resumable writer creates or renames groups and adds active members whose Elvanto person IDs are known. Only group names and memberships map; descriptions, custom group fields, meeting details, local waitlists/paused statuses, and role values other than Leader or Assistant Leader are not exported. Existing Elvanto-only members are retained, and member removals are left for manual review. Each batch makes at most five API writes and checks no more than 100 preview operations. An uncertain API write invalidates the preview to avoid blind retries. Outbound group links are stored separately from imported source markers so local groups remain editable.

A read-only Services preview pages through up to 200,000 Elvanto services, expands service times into individual local events, and reports new events, existing source links, same-title/date conflicts, invalid rows, and up to 50 sample actions. Each Elvanto service/time pair uses a stable composite source ID. Plans map to the local event time plan; volunteer positions map to event positions and link to a contact only when that Elvanto person was imported already. A complete preview less than one hour old is required before import. Import processes at most five API pages per request and saves progress transactionally for resuming. Existing imported events refresh from Elvanto; same-title/date local events are skipped for manual resolution. Imported Elvanto events and assignments are read-only through normal event, attendance, check-in, and position-response workflows. Events absent from Elvanto are not deleted. This adapter is one-way and does not yet map standalone songs/arrangements, service series, event registrations, or bidirectional edits.

A read-only Calendar preview accepts an administrator-selected inclusive date range and reports new events, existing source links, same-title/date conflicts, invalid rows, and sample actions. It reads up to 200,000 records across the selected range and requires a complete preview less than one hour old before import. Import proceeds in batches of at most five pages and resumes transactionally. Elvanto event IDs are stored with a `calendar:` source prefix to distinguish them from service/time IDs. Event title, date, time, location, and description map to the local schedule. Elvanto returns calendar timestamps in UTC; all-day entries display at midnight because the local event model has no all-day or multi-day fields. Private admin notes are not imported. Imported calendar entries are read-only and are not deleted when absent from Elvanto. The current adapter is one-way and does not cover reverse-direction edits or incremental change detection.

A read-only People Flows preview fetches flow definitions, nested steps, descriptions, instructions, due settings, and step assignments/statuses. It reports new and previously imported flows, steps, and assignment totals before writes. A complete preview expires after one hour and is required to import. The importer upserts location-scoped flow snapshots in batches of five flows and saves a cursor for resuming. Administrators can inspect imported steps, instructions, due settings, and assignment status; assignment person IDs show their local contact name when the People adapter has linked that person. Stable Elvanto flow, step, person, and assignment IDs are retained; assignments are not merged with local workflow records because Smart Church does not yet have a matching workflow domain model. Imported snapshots are read-only and flows absent from Elvanto are not deleted. The importer currently caps previews at 500 flows, 2,000 steps, and 20,000 assignments per run; the Elvanto assignment endpoint does not provide pagination.
A read-only Songs preview scans the complete paginated song list and compares source IDs with stored snapshots. A complete preview expires after one hour and is required to import. Songs import in resumable batches of five; each snapshot contains the song metadata plus its arrangements and associated keys, including API-provided lyrics and chord charts. Administrators can inspect imported song details and arrangements. Imported snapshots are read-only and songs absent from Elvanto are not deleted. Elvanto’s file payloads are intentionally excluded. This does not yet create a native Smart Church song library or service-plan song picker.

A read-only financial preview requires the separate site-admin financial authorization and an explicitly selected inclusive date range. It scans up to 200,000 giving transactions and stores a complete stable-ID preview for one hour. Import resumes in batches of up to five API pages; each record keeps the original transaction, donor, batch, method, amount/category, and memo fields inside an AES-GCM encrypted payload. Only site administrators can list or inspect imported records, and reads are denied whenever the financial authorization is disabled. The importer is read-only, leaves records absent upstream intact, and does not merge donor records into People or EventDesk finance. Each resumed batch checks its transaction IDs against the stored preview before writing. The Elvanto financial endpoint requires a start and end date, so admins must choose the full history window they want.

Read-only Elvanto API calls retry network timeouts, HTTP 429 responses, and server errors up to two times, honoring `Retry-After` when supplied and capping each wait at ten seconds. Create, edit, and remove calls are not automatically retried because the outcome may be uncertain after a timeout and retrying a write could create duplicates. If a People write fails after it was sent, the saved cursor remains in place and the preview is invalidated; run a fresh preview before sending another write.

After choosing directions, an administrator can check read access for each selected area; each permission check fetches at most one record per list endpoint, and results contain only access status and a count. Songs also checks the arrangements endpoint when a song is available. Calendar and financial checks query a rolling five-year window. No records from these permission checks are stored. Direction choices do not activate a background job. People and Groups now have reviewed, resumable exports as well as imports, but only the explicitly mapped fields described above are synchronized. Services, Calendar, People Flows, Songs, and financial transactions still have limited one-way imports. Other data areas are not currently imported, exported, or synchronized; a saved direction does not mean that direction is implemented.

## Set up a connection

1. Sign in to Elvanto with an administrator account.
2. Find the Secret API Key under **Settings → Developers → API**. Elvanto's [API key help article](https://help.elvanto.com/hc/en-us/articles/7626452924823-How-to-use-the-Elvanto-API) documents this location.
3. In Smart Church, open **Settings → Modules → Sync** for the location and paste the key into the Elvanto section. The key is sent to the API server for verification and encrypted at rest. It is never returned to the browser after saving.
4. Choose directions for each data area. Financial/giving starts disabled and needs its own explicit authorization.
5. Select **Test connection and save**. This validates the API key and People read permission; it does not test permissions for the other areas.
6. Select **Check selected API permissions** to check read access for every enabled direction. This is read-only and does not validate write access.
7. For Elvanto-to-Smart-Church People sync, select **Preview People import**, review the counts and email matches, then select **Import eligible contacts**. Import proceeds in batches of five pages; use **Continue People import** until the saved progress is complete. Later, rerun the preview and use **Refresh People from Elvanto** to reprocess the People list. Email matches are skipped for manual resolution.
8. For Smart-Church-to-Elvanto People sync, select **Preview People export**, review the create/update counts and conflicts, then select **Export People to Elvanto**. Continue the export until saved progress is complete. The export does not modify Elvanto volunteer/contact flags, family data, custom fields, or records absent from Smart Church.
9. Select **Preview Groups import** and review name conflicts and linked members. Import or refresh proceeds in batches of five pages. Import People first if you want the Groups adapter to link its members. For Smart-Church-to-Elvanto sync, select **Preview Groups export**, review the group and membership actions, then export. Use **Continue Groups export** to resume; it remains available after reloading the page. Only group names and active links to Elvanto People are exported. Elvanto-only memberships are kept, and member removals are never automatic.
10. Select **Preview Services import** and review event conflicts, service dates, and the sample list. Import or refresh proceeds in batches of five pages. Import People first to link Elvanto volunteers to local contacts.
11. Choose Calendar start and end dates, select **Preview Calendar import**, and review the returned records and conflicts. Import proceeds in batches of five pages and only covers that date range.
12. Select **Preview People Flows import**, review flow, step, and assignment totals, then import. Continue the import until all flows are saved. Import People first if you want to use the retained person IDs to link these assignments later.
13. Select **Preview Songs import**, review the song count, then import. Continue in batches until complete; open an imported song to inspect arrangements, keys, lyrics, and charts.
14. Enable the separate financial-data authorization as a site administrator. Choose the complete giving date range, preview the transaction count, and import. Imported donor and payment information remains encrypted and visible only while that authorization is enabled.

Elvanto documents API-key authentication using HTTP Basic authentication, with the API key as the username and a dummy password, in its [Getting Started guide](https://www.elvanto.com/api/getting-started/).

## Scope still to implement and verify

- Full People and volunteer field/status/custom-field mapping, native financial ledgers/receipts, and historical live-account verification. People Flows, Songs, and financial transactions have read-only snapshot importers, while Services and Calendar have one-way import slices; native People Flows workflows, a native song library and service-plan song picker, additional service/calendar metadata, and reverse-direction writes remain open.
- Per-area field, relationship, custom-field, status, and deletion mappings; broader dry-run previews; incremental sync; and resumable pagination for every area. People import resumes by page but does not detect changes after its initial import.
- Conflict policy, deletion/archive behavior, rate-limit handling, retries, audit history, and disconnection after imports exist.
- Non-production read/write verification for every area and direction. The permission check covers selected-area read access only; it does not verify write access or perform a synchronization.

No Elvanto credentials are committed. Do not paste API keys into source files, issues, or chat.
